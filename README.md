# Project 5
## Implementation / Features you added
-For a theme/narrative of the game the player is a scientist who got locked into their workplace, a sterile and winding lab, and by avoiding moving minerals they are able to escape.
-there are two main enemies to avoid, and ice capsule, and a marble pillar which the player can destroy by clicking. They mainly function as obstacles 
- the two primary light sources are the spotlight attached to the player functioning as a flashlight, and the directional light to create a sense of darkness in the maze
- there is a shimmering noise added to the exit key to add an odd ambiance to the scene, the audio is pulled from https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/sparkle/ (shimmering object: freesound_community) 
- the materials used were a wood texture applied to the floors, a plastic material applied to make the walls seem odd and weirdly clean, an ice material to the capsule, and a marble one added to the pillar, and finally an alluminum material added to the cube
- there are two primary levels to the mazes as well as a beginning and ending screen
